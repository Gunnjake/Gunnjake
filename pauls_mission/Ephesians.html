Ephesians

On Unity, Grace, and the Body of Christ

AI's Overview of Ephesians

Paul’s Letter to the Ephesians emphasizes the unity of believers in Christ and the transformative power of God’s grace. Written while Paul was imprisoned, it highlights how salvation is a gift from God, not earned through works, and calls believers to live in harmony as one body. The letter also provides practical guidance on relationships, spiritual warfare, and standing firm in faith.
