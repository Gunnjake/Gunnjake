Why Paul and His Letters Matter to Me

When I was serving my mission in the Philippines, I had a hard time adjusting to everything, as I am sure many other missionaries felt as well. Slowly, I was able to adjust and embrace everything. Throughout the hard times, my dad took various work trips to the cities where Paul preached. Every time he would visit those cities, he would send me a verse from the corresponding letter that uplifted me.

The one that stuck out to me the most was when I was in my last area and getting ready to go home. I was struggling. I felt like I still had work to do, and I doubted that I did everything I could and wondered if there was more that I could have done. My Dad sent me the scripture 2 Timothy 4:7, "I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith." This scripture resonated with me and made me realize that I had served a good mission and I put up a good fight, but most importantly, I kept my faith in our Savior, Jesus Christ.
